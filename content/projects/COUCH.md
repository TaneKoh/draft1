Created a motorized rig that could be controlled using a PS4 controller. I designed a circuit to safely power and control two 1800W bi-directional DC motors, and then reconfigured the throttle and motor speed systems to be controlled by an ESP32 through DAC pins. Our mechanical team then constructed a chassis and chain drive system.
